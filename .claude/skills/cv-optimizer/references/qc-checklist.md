# QC checklist (report pass / fail per line, fix failures before delivering)

## Evidence and accuracy
- [ ] Every number and claim traces to the original CV or a user answer.
- [ ] No technology, certification, title or employer was added without evidence.
- [ ] Dates, titles and tenure are consistent and recomputed (`fit_score.py tenure`).
- [ ] Estimates are labelled as estimates.
- [ ] Wording does not over-claim (service vs. asset, productivity vs. formal metrics, AI-assisted vs. autonomous).

## Relevance
- [ ] Headline and summary match the target role family.
- [ ] The strongest evidence for the role's top requirements appears in the top third of page one.
- [ ] Keywords from the job description appear where genuinely true, without stuffing or repetition.

## Quality
- [ ] Bullets follow Action + Scope + Technology + Impact; no weak verbs.
- [ ] Grammar, tense (past for old roles, present for current), and consistent number formats.
- [ ] No unnecessary repetition across bullets or sections.
- [ ] Skills section is grouped, ordered by relevance, and defensible in an interview.

## Format
- [ ] <= 2 pages, single column, standard headings, ATS-friendly (no tables for core content, no text in images).
- [ ] Contact details present and consistent with the profile.

## Recruiter test (answer in one line each)
1. Who is this candidate?
2. Why are they relevant to this role?
3. What value would they bring?

## Open items
- [ ] Every `[TO CONFIRM]` is resolved or removed from the text.
