# Writing rules of thumb

## Experience bullets
- Pattern: **Action + Scope + Technology + Impact** (use at least three of the four).
- One primary achievement per bullet, 25-45 words (hard max about 50), 1-2 metrics, 2-4 technologies.
- 5-8 bullets for the most relevant role; fewer for older ones. Consolidate related items.
- Strong verbs: architected, designed, engineered; built, developed, implemented; led, mentored, established;
  optimized, accelerated, reduced; automated, orchestrated, streamlined; hardened, validated, standardized;
  shipped, delivered, deployed.
- Avoid: responsible for, worked on, helped with, participated in, involved in, assisted with, was part of.
- Do not upgrade "contributed to" to "architected" unless the candidate owned the architecture.

## Numbers
- Prefer before and after (`from 1-2 days to 2-4 hours`); include scale (people, teams, items).
- Keep "estimated" when a baseline was estimated, not measured.
- Consistent formats: `11+ engineers`, `75/75 items`, `2-3 hours`, `20 -> 45 FPS`.
- Never invent a percentage; only derive one from clear underlying figures.

## Summary (60-100 words)
Opens with professional identity and accurate tenure, 3-5 relevant capabilities, 1-2 standout achievements, a factual
tone, no first person, no "passionate / results-driven / team player".

## Headline
Lead with the identity the target role wants. For individual-contributor roles keep management titles in the
experience section.

## Skills
- Hierarchy: languages -> target-role specialisation -> AI/domain -> backend/frontend -> infra/cloud -> testing/tooling
  -> databases.
- Most relevant first; no self-rated levels; no duplicates across categories; compact (6-8 groups max).
- Only technologies the candidate can discuss. Name exactly what is evidenced.

## Tenure and labels
- Compute years from dates (`fit_score.py tenure`). "Nearly 4 years" beats "3+" when it is true.
- Never count unrelated service as engineering experience.
- Keep the real title and domain; add one factual descriptor line under the employer if it helps the target role.

## ATS and layout
Single column, standard section names (Summary, Skills, Experience, Education), no core content in tables or images,
plain bullets, consistent date format, <= 2 pages. Use job-description terms naturally and only when accurate.

## Master CV
Never overwrite it. Create one tailored variant per role family and keep a changelog.
